Routinist is a social running app for people who want to make daily running a habit — with friends.

🏃 Automatic tracking
- Sync with Health Connect (Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Polar)
- Built-in GPS tracking for distance, pace, route

🏆 Motivation that works
- Daily / weekly / monthly leaderboards
- Mileage rewards (1km = 100 mileage)
- Monthly challenges + virtual world marathons (Boston, NYC)

📸 Running social feed
- Share photos + a one-line quote
- Neighborhood / friends / club feeds
- Auto-generated MP4 share cards with pace animation

👥 Clubs & friends
- Create or join running clubs
- Club world runs (solo or summed)
- 1:1 chat + cheer messages

🛍 Reward shop
- Running gear, shoes, nutrition
- Pay with mileage + Toss / KakaoPay / NaverPay

Start running together. Start with Routinist.
