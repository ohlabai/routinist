v1.2.21 — Your share card now has a real map

[Share card]
• The top of the card is now a real map, so anyone can see where you ran.
• Text over the map is dark ink — readable without hiding the map underneath.
• A loading note appears while the map arrives, and sharing waits until it's ready.

[Voice cues]
• Your split pace is now called out as an animal. Fast is "cheetah fast", steady is "turtle steady — that's the strongest kind".
• Phrases alternate so you don't hear the same line every kilometre.

[Apple Watch]
• Fixed the start button not responding after you set a goal.
• The goal screen fits on one page, and the Done button no longer covers what's above it.
• Fewer cases where voice cues were silent on your earphones or watch speaker.

[Clubs]
• Club races are here. Run in pairs and your two times are added together for the ranking.
• Just tap join before the start — runs during the race window are timed automatically.
• Organisers can enter times by hand for participants who don't use the app.

[Records & ranking]
• Fixed the same run being saved twice.
• Your region is now detected from where you run, so you appear in the ranking even without setting it.
• Fixed finishing onboarding but still being left out of the national ranking.

[Notifications]
• Winner picks, personal bests and invite rewards now stay in your inbox — you can find them even if you miss the push.
• Winner pick notifications can be turned off in settings.
