v1.2.8 — Weekly streaks, a simpler app, instant celebration

[Weekly running streak]
• Streaks now count by week, not by day — perfect for 2-3 runs-a-week runners.
• Set your weekly goal with one tap on Home and watch the day dots fill up.
• A gentle weekend nudge when your streak needs one more run, plus streak protection for busy weeks.

[Celebrate right after your run]
• New badges and mileage rewards now appear the moment you save a run.
• See "Run #n this week" at a glance.
• We also celebrate your imported runs right after connecting Apple Health.

[A simpler app]
• Bottom navigation trimmed to 4 tabs; Social now focuses on Friends and Photos.
• New runners see only the cards that matter.

[Improvements and fixes]
• Run of the Day and monthly pacemaker matching are now more reliable.
• Tapping a notification takes you to the right screen, and you can check items one by one in the inbox.
• More notification categories so you only get what you want.
