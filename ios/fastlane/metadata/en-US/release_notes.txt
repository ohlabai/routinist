v1.2.17 — Smarter auto-pause

[Run tracking]
• Fixed auto-pause kicking in mid-run — we now watch your body's motion too, so weak GPS spots (tall buildings, tunnels) won't stop your run while you're moving.
• Fixed the watch too — no more pause/resume loop while GPS locks on right after you start.
• Pocketing your phone during the start countdown no longer prevents recording.
• It pauses only when you actually stop, and picks right back up when you run again.

[Share card]
• Monthly run count now counts runs of 3 km or more — short strolls won't inflate the number.

[More]
• Polish and stability improvements.
