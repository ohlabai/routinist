v1.2.20 — Three fixes found on an actual run

[Auto-pause]
• Fixed runs resuming on their own while you were still walking. It now waits until you're really running again.
• Resume is judged from the path you actually covered rather than a single GPS speed reading, so a noisy signal no longer triggers it.

[Voice cues]
• Fixed the first word of a voice cue being cut off while music or video was playing.

[Lock screen]
• Distance and time now show correctly on your iPhone lock screen during an Apple Watch run — the phone app was clearing the watch's run display.
