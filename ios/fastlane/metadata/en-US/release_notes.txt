v1.2.13 — Apple Watch app, one-tap cheers, original photo view

[Apple Watch app is here ⌚]
• Start a run right from your wrist - time, distance, pace and heart rate live.
• Finished runs save to Apple Health and flow into Routinist on your iPhone automatically.
• Leave your phone at home - the watch records on its own.

[Faster cheers]
• Tap the cheer button to send a heart instantly. Hold it to pick another emoji.
• Multiple PBs from one run now arrive as a single bundled notification.

[Original photo view]
• Tap a shared card to see the clean photo without the stats. Hold to peek.
• Your own photos can be saved in their original form.
• Tap the share card preview to pick a background photo right away.

[Friend requests]
• Adding a friend now follows request → accept everywhere, with a clear "requested" state.

[Voice guidance]
• Clearer number pronunciation for distances beyond 10km.

[Sync]
• Health data now refreshes automatically every 6 hours - no manual refresh needed.
