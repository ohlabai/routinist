v1.2.5 — International GPS Recovery + Expanded Region Labels

[GPS Recovery for International Marathons / Long Runs]
• Fixed a regression where large, long activities were dropped when loading 90 days of GPS routes at once.
• GPS routes are now matched in 30-day chunks, with an automatic retry split if a chunk runs out of time.
• Long international runs (Switzerland, Spain, Portugal, etc.) now show their full GPS route on share cards.

[Region Label City Expansion]
• Switzerland +13: Bern, Basel, Lausanne, Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt, Grindelwald, St. Moritz, Davos, Locarno, Lugano, Montreux, Winterthur.
• Spain +14: Santiago de Compostela, Seville, Valencia, Málaga, Granada, Bilbao, San Sebastián, Mallorca, Ibiza, Tenerife, Gran Canaria, etc.
• Portugal +7: Sintra, Cascais, Coimbra, Faro, Lagos, Madeira, Azores.
• Offshore islands beyond the mainland bbox (Tenerife, Mallorca, Madeira, etc.) are now labeled correctly too.

[Home Calendar]
• When you have multiple activities on the same day, the one with a GPS map is now picked first.
• Previously a later short activity could open first and hide the map.

Your routes and region names now show up correctly when running abroad.
