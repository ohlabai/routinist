Review guide (v1.2.18 build 349)

1. Sign in: use the demo account above, or Sign in with Apple.

2. Guideline 1.2 (UGC) — response to the Aug 5, 2026 rejection (Submission b8addbe4). All five required precautions are implemented:
- EULA: users must check an explicit consent checkbox on the sign-in screen before signing in or registering; sign-in is blocked until consent. Terms Article 5 states a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive users (https://routinist.kr/terms).
- Filtering: all user-generated text (comments, direct messages, quotes, club names, profile names/bios) must pass a server-side objectionable-word filter before being stored.
- Flagging: report buttons with reason categories on every UGC surface — photos, photo comments, activity comments, quotes, user profiles, direct messages.
- Blocking: available from the photo report dialog, user profiles, and chat. Blocking instantly removes the blocked user's content from the blocker's feed (enforced server-side), prevents further messages, and automatically notifies our operations team.
- 24-hour response: every report/block immediately pushes a notification to our operations team; the admin console removes the content and can delete the offending account. The 24-hour commitment is stated in our Terms.

3. Background audio (re: prior 2.5.4 review): UIBackgroundModes "audio" is used solely for TTS voice coaching during an active GPS run. Start a run (Home > Start), lock the device, and move — distance/pace cues play in the background every km; stopping plays an "auto-paused" cue, moving again plays "resumed". The app does NOT activate an audio session when merely launched.

4. HealthKit (2.5.1 resolution, unchanged): "Apple Health Connected" chip with Sync button at the top of Home after sign-in; full card under My Info > Health App Connect. NSHealthShareUsageDescription lists the data read (distance, duration, calories, heart rate, pace) and purposes (statistics, personal bests, ranking).

5. Account deletion: My Info > "Delete account" > warning > type confirmation > permanent erase.

6. Mileage / Shop: mileage is auto-earned from running distance and spent only on physical running goods in the in-app shop (My Info > Shop). No digital goods or subscriptions, so IAP is not required (3.1.1 physical-goods exception; payments via Toss Payments PG).

7. Permissions: HealthKit / Location (GPS tracking, regional ranking) / Photo Library (photo upload, share-card save) / Camera (photo capture) / Notifications (cheers, reports).
